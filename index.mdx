## Who Nestlyze is for

Nestlyze is built for US homebuyers who want a second opinion that isn't paid by the seller. It's most useful if you are:

- **Actively shopping** and want to compare homes across cities or school districts.
- **Evaluating a specific address** — a listing you saw on Zillow, Redfin, or in person.
- **New to a market** and need an honest read on price, condition, and risk.

If you're a first-time buyer, start with [Quickstart](/quickstart). If you already have an address in mind, jump to [Analyze an address](/features/property-analysis).
