Your Nestlyze profile is the foundation of your recommendations. The answers you provided during onboarding — budget, commute location, must-haves, deal-breakers — are stored in your buyer profile and used every time the AI selects and ranks homes for you. Keeping that profile current means your recommendations stay relevant as your situation changes.

## Dashboard overview

When you open the Dashboard, you see three quick-access tiles at the top:

- **Picks / Onboard** — if you've completed onboarding, this takes you straight to your 24 AI-matched recommendations. If not, it starts the 2-minute onboarding chat.
- **Shortlist** — shows how many homes you've saved and links to the comparison view.
- **Browse** — opens the full listing pool with filters.

Below those tiles, the Dashboard shows:

- **Saved searches** — any filter sets you've saved from the Browse view, with alert toggles
- **What's new** — the freshest listings across the pool added in the past 7 days
- **Recently viewed** — listings you've opened recently, stored locally in your browser
- **My reports** — your full history of deep property analyses, searchable by address

## Editing your buyer profile

Your buyer profile is built from your answers during the onboarding chat. To update it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your profile page">
    Navigate to `/onboard` or click the **Picks** tile on your Dashboard. If you've already completed onboarding, you'll be taken to your current recommendations. Re-run the onboarding chat to update your answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the onboarding questions again">
    The 2-minute chat covers budget range, target cities, commute address, school priorities, and lifestyle preferences. Your previous answers are replaced when you complete a new session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See refreshed recommendations">
    Once the new profile is saved, your 24 recommendations are regenerated using your updated priorities. The AI re-ranks and re-selects homes based on the new inputs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your budget or target city changes, re-running onboarding is the fastest way to get a fresh set of relevant listings.
</Tip>

## How profile changes affect recommendations

Your profile affects recommendations in two ways:

1. **Filtering** — homes outside your stated budget or city preferences are excluded from the pool before ranking begins.
2. **Ranking** — within the eligible pool, homes are ranked by how closely they match your commute, school, lifestyle, and space requirements. A 2-sentence explanation on each card tells you exactly which factors drove the match.

More complete answers produce better-ranked results. Vague or minimal answers result in a broader but less targeted list.

## Report sharing toggle

Each analysis report can be shared at two levels. You control this per-report from the Share modal:

- **Private share link** — anyone who has the link can view the report. No account required to view.
- **Public / indexable** — an additional opt-in that lets Nestlyze index the report so it can appear in search results when someone searches that address. This is a separate consent step from creating the share link.

You can revoke either level at any time from the Share modal on the report.

## Saved searches and alert preferences

From the Browse view, you can save any filter combination as a named search. Saved searches appear on your Dashboard and can have email alerts toggled on or off per search. When alerts are on, Nestlyze emails you when new listings matching that filter set appear.

To toggle alerts for a saved search:

1. Open the Dashboard.
2. Locate the saved search under **Saved searches**.
3. Click the bell icon to toggle alerts on or off.

To delete a saved search, click the trash icon next to it.

## Language settings

Nestlyze supports English, Chinese (Simplified), and Spanish. Switch languages using the language selector in the top-right corner of the Settings page. The interface, reports, and AI-generated content all update to the selected language.

## Account deletion and data export

To delete your account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to `/settings` and scroll to the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete account">
    Confirm the prompt. Nestlyze deletes your profile, reports, shortlist, and authentication record within 48 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent. Your profile, onboarding answers, and analysis history cannot be recovered after the 48-hour deletion window completes.
</Warning>

Any share links you created remain accessible for 30 days after account deletion unless you manually revoke them first from the Share modal on each report.
