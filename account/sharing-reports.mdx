---
title: "Share Your Property Analysis Report"
description: "Share a Nestlyze analysis report with your partner, agent, or family via a private link or make it discoverable in the public library."
---

After running a deep analysis, you can share the full report with anyone — a partner, your buyer's agent, a family member — without requiring them to have a Nestlyze account. You can also opt in to make the report publicly discoverable so other buyers researching the same address can find it.

## How to share a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    Navigate to the analysis report you want to share. You can find all your past reports under **My reports** on the Dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Share">
    Click the **Share** button. The Share modal opens with a pre-generated share link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or send the link">
    Copy the share link and send it however you prefer — text message, email, WhatsApp, or any other channel. The Share modal also includes one-click buttons for WhatsApp, SMS, X, and LinkedIn.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Anyone who opens the share link can view the full report — composite score, all 6 agent sections, Nestimate valuation, and analysis details. They do not need to create an account or spend a credit to view a shared report.

## Two sharing levels

Nestlyze distinguishes between two types of report visibility, because sharing with a friend and sharing with the internet are meaningfully different.

### Private share link

Creating a share link is the default action. The link is unique to your report and anyone who has it can view the report. The report is **not** indexed by search engines — it cannot be found by someone searching for the address on Google.

Use this when you want to send the report to a specific person.

### Public / indexable

If you want to make the report discoverable — so that other Nestlyze users or people searching for that address can find it — you can opt in to public indexing. This is a separate, explicit step:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Share modal">
    Click **Share** on the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the public library opt-in">
    At the bottom of the Share modal, check the box labeled **List in the Nestlyze public report library**. The description confirms: search engines can index this report so others find it via Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The change saves immediately. A confirmation message appears. You can uncheck the box at any time to remove the report from the public library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Public indexing is entirely optional and opt-in. Your reports are private by default and only become discoverable if you explicitly enable this setting.
</Note>

## Revoking sharing

To stop sharing a report:

- **Private link** — open the Share modal and the link can be regenerated. Older links to the previous token will stop working.
- **Public / indexable** — open the Share modal and uncheck the **List in the Nestlyze public report library** option. The report is removed from the public index. Search engines will de-index it over their normal crawl cycle.

If you delete your account, all share links remain accessible for 30 days unless you revoke them first.

## Sharing a shortlist

You can also share a shortlist of up to 3 homes at once. The shortlist share generates a single link that shows a side-by-side comparison view of the saved homes. This is useful for walking through options with a partner or agent without sharing individual reports one at a time.

To share your shortlist, open the **Compare** page and use the share option at the top.

## PDF export and email report

In addition to link-based sharing, you can get a report in two other formats:

- **PDF export** — generates a downloadable PDF of the full report. Use the export option on any report page.
- **Email report** — enter a recipient's email address in the Share modal to send the report directly. The recipient receives the full report content in a formatted email. No Nestlyze account is required to read it.
