Nestlyze uses a simple credit system: one credit equals one deep property analysis. Your account starts with one free credit — no payment information required. When you need more analyses, you buy a credit pack through Stripe's secure checkout.

## What's always free

You never spend credits on the following features:

- **AI recommendations** — your personalized list of 24 matched homes
- **Ask-AI** — follow-up questions on any report or listing
- **Nestimate on listing cards** — valuation ranges shown while browsing
- **Browsing and map** — explore the full listing pool and map view
- **Shortlist and compare** — save and compare up to 3 homes

Credits are only consumed when you run a full deep property analysis on a specific address.

## How to buy credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your avatar or navigate to **Settings** from any page. You can also reach it at `/settings`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a credit pack">
    Scroll to the **Deep reports** section. Select the Starter, Popular, or Pro pack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure checkout page. Pay by card. Stripe processes the payment and credits are added to your account instantly upon confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After a successful purchase, your credit balance updates immediately in the Settings page header.

## Redemption codes

If you have a promotional or referral code, enter it in the **Redeem code** field in Settings. Credits are applied to your account right away and your updated balance is shown in the confirmation message.

<Tip>
  Every Nestlyze account has a unique referral code. Each friend who signs up using your link earns you one additional credit, with no cap.
</Tip>

## Commercial property detection

Nestlyze is designed for US residential properties. When you submit an address, the system checks whether it is residential before running the analysis. If the address is detected as commercial, the analysis stops and your credit is automatically refunded — you are not charged for a report that couldn't be completed.

<Info>
  Condos, townhomes, and multi-family residences are all supported. Only addresses that are clearly non-residential (office buildings, retail, industrial) trigger a refund.
</Info>

## Rate limits

Authenticated users have their own dedicated request bucket, so your analyses are not affected by other users' activity. Unauthenticated free-trial requests share a common IP-based bucket.

## Refunds for unused credits

Email [hello@nestlyze.com](mailto:hello@nestlyze.com) within 14 days of purchase to request a refund of any unused credits. Credits that have already been used to generate a report are not refundable.

If an analysis fails entirely — for example, if a data source is unavailable and the full report cannot be generated — your credit is automatically refunded without you needing to contact support.
