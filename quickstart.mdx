Nestlyze is an AI buyer's agent that does the research a traditional buyer's agent does in days — comparable sales, school-district boundaries, climate hazards, permit history, financial projections — and delivers it in about 60 seconds. You don't need an account to start. Choose the path that matches where you are right now: you already have an address in mind, or you want the AI to surface homes for you.

<Note>
  Your first property analysis is completely free. No credit card, no account required. You can run the analysis, read the full report, and save it to your dashboard after signing up — all at no cost.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Analyze an address">
    Use this path when you have a specific home in mind — a listing you found on Zillow, a house you drove past, or an address a friend recommended. Nestlyze dispatches six AI domain experts against that one address and returns a composite score with domain-level detail in about 60 seconds.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to nestlyze.com and enter the address">
        On the Nestlyze home page, find the **"I already have an address"** shortcut beneath the primary call-to-action button. Type or paste the full US residential street address — including city and state — into the input field. Nestlyze uses Google Geocoding to confirm the address before starting; if the autocomplete dropdown offers a suggestion, select it to make sure the address resolves correctly.

        Once you submit, the page transitions to the analysis view immediately. Single-address analysis works for any residential property nationwide, even if the address is outside the primary listing pool.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Watch the progress panel while 6 AI agents run">
        The progress panel shows you exactly what is happening in real time. Six domain agents run in parallel, each working a distinct area of due diligence:

        - **Market** — comparable sales, price trend, Nestimate valuation range
        - **Physical** — structure age, permit history, listing condition signals, satellite and street-view inspection
        - **Financial** — five-year cost projection, tax reassessment after sale, HOA status, insurance estimate
        - **Environmental** — FEMA flood zone, Cal Fire wildfire tier, USGS seismic risk, air quality, contamination site proximity
        - **Neighborhood** — Walk/Transit/Bike scores, school-attendance boundary lookup, crime data, nearby amenities
        - **Legal** — title signals, zoning, HOA financial health, active code violations

        A rotating status message and a percentage counter keep you oriented. Within about 15 seconds a property preview card appears — a Street View image of the home plus the geocoded address and basic specs — confirming Nestlyze resolved the right property. The full analysis typically completes in 1–2 minutes.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Read the composite score and domain cards">
        When all six agents finish, the report view loads. At the top you will find a **composite score out of 100** — a weighted synthesis of the six domain scores — alongside an executive summary written by a separate synthesizer model that draws on all six reports.

        Below the summary, six domain cards expand the detail for each area. Each card shows:

        - The domain score (0–100) color-coded from red through amber to green
        - A one-paragraph expert summary citing the actual data points used
        - Key findings ranked by significance

        Scroll through all six cards before drawing conclusions. A strong composite score can coexist with one weak domain that matters a great deal to your specific priorities.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Interpret deal-breaker banners">
        Nestlyze flags hard stops in a prominent banner at the top of the report. A deal-breaker banner appears when any of the following are confirmed: a title defect, an active code violation, or a financially insolvent HOA. When a deal-breaker banner is present, the composite score is capped at 40 regardless of how the other domains score.

        Deal-breaker banners are distinct from risk-flag callouts, which appear inline within individual domain cards. Risk flags (for example, a high flood-risk designation or a pending special assessment) are important but do not automatically cap the score — they are inputs for your judgment and for discussion with your buyer's agent.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Save the report to your dashboard">
        At the bottom of the report, click **"Save to dashboard"**. If you are not yet signed in, Nestlyze will prompt you to create a free account (email or Google). Once saved, the report appears on your dashboard alongside any other addresses you have analyzed, and you can export a PDF to share with your buyer's agent or co-buyer.

        You can also use the share button to generate a shareable link. Reports are private by default; you can optionally make a report publicly indexable if you want others to find it.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Find matching homes">
    Use this path when you do not yet have a specific address — you want Nestlyze to narrow a pool of 6,000+ active listings down to 24 that genuinely fit your life. The AI builds a buyer profile from a short conversational onboarding and then scores every listing against your priorities.

    <Tip>
      The onboarding chat works completely anonymously — no account required. Nestlyze stores your profile in your browser using a session token. You only need to create an account if you want to save your recommendations across devices, receive price-drop alerts, or pick up where you left off on a different browser or phone.
    </Tip>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click 'Talk to your agent' on the home page">
        From the Nestlyze home page, click the **"Talk to your agent"** button — the primary call-to-action in the hero section. This opens the onboarding chat at `nestlyze.com/onboard`.

        If you have used Nestlyze before, a resume banner appears at the top of the chat. The banner shows how many facts and preferences are already in your profile and gives you three options: continue with what Nestlyze already knows, update one specific detail, or start fresh. Pick **"Find me homes with what you have"** to go straight to recommendations without repeating the chat.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Complete the onboarding chat">
        The chat opens with a greeting from your AI buyer's agent and a text input at the bottom. If you are not sure what to say, tap one of the **starter chips** displayed above the input — for example:

        - *"I work in San Francisco — ideally less than 30 min commute."*
        - *"Family of 4: 2 adults, 2 kids in elementary school."*
        - *"Budget around $1.5M, first-time buyer."*
        - *"Just exploring — not in a rush yet."*

        Tapping a starter chip pre-fills the text field so you can edit it before sending. You can respond in English, Chinese, or Spanish — Nestlyze follows your language automatically.

        The agent asks follow-up questions to extract your work location, commute tolerance, household composition, budget, timeline, school priorities, walkability preferences, climate risk tolerance, and neighborhood style. You do not need to answer every question — the chat is conversational, not a form. After a few exchanges, a **confidence bar** at the top of the chat fills toward 100 %. Once confidence reaches about 50 %, the "Skip" button in the top-right corner changes to **"I'm done — show picks"**, signaling that Nestlyze has enough to work with.

        The right-hand sidebar (on desktop) shows your profile being built in real time: facts such as work location and budget, preferences such as school priority, and inferred implications the AI has drawn from what you said.

        The whole conversation typically takes about two minutes.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Watch confidence build, then click 'See my homes'">
        As you respond, the violet progress bar at the top of the chat panel fills gradually. Each turn adds to Nestlyze's confidence that it understands your priorities well enough to produce meaningful recommendations.

        When the agent determines it has collected enough signal, it sends a completion message and the input area is replaced by a large **"Great — I have enough to start. Find me homes →"** button. Click it to proceed. If you are signed in, Nestlyze routes you directly to your recommendations. If you are anonymous, a sign-up prompt appears offering Google sign-in or email — you can skip this and your profile stays active on your current device.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Browse your 24 narrative-annotated recommendations">
        The recommendations page loads a set of 24 homes matched to your profile. At the top, an italicized summary paragraph explains how Nestlyze interpreted your priorities and which regions it searched. Below that, your 24 picks appear in a grid of cards.

        Each card shows:

        - A listing photo or Street View image
        - The fit score (0–100) in a color-coded badge — green for 80+, blue for 65–79, amber for 50–64
        - Price, beds, baths, and square footage
        - Commute time to your work location
        - A region label, with a **"NEW AREA"** badge on any region Nestlyze surfaced that a local agent would be unlikely to suggest

        Below the basic specs, each card has a **narrative annotation** labeled "AI match" — one or two sentences written specifically about this home against your profile. For example: *"This 4bd/2ba in Willow Glen hits your 25-min commute window via Hwy 17 and sits inside the Schallenberger Elementary zone (9/10). You'll pay about 4 % over your cap, but the recent $85K price cut already closes most of that gap."* This narrative cites your actual inputs — your commute anchor, budget cap, school priority — against the listing's real data.

        Use the **region filter buttons** at the top of the list to focus on a specific area. Use the **sort dropdown** to re-order by price, newest build, or largest square footage instead of best match. Use the **"Adjust match weights"** panel to drag sliders and re-rank by emphasizing commute, schools, price, or other factors — Nestlyze re-picks the 24 with your new weights after a short delay.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Open a listing detail page">
        Click any card to open the full listing detail page for that home. The detail page shows:

        - All listing photos
        - Walk Score, Transit Score, and Bike Score
        - FEMA flood zone, Cal Fire wildfire tier, and seismic risk level
        - School assignment from the actual attendance-boundary polygon (not just nearest schools)
        - A side-by-side comparison against the neighborhood median price per square foot
        - A five-year ownership cost projection

        From the detail page you can run a full single-address analysis — the same six-agent deep dive described in the "Analyze an address" path — to get a composite score and a report you can share with your buyer's agent.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
