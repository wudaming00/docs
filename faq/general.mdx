Below are answers to the questions we hear most often. If something isn't covered here, email [hello@nestlyze.com](mailto:hello@nestlyze.com) and we'll get back to you.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Nestlyze?">
    Nestlyze is an AI buyer's agent for US homebuyers. Tell us about yourself in a 2-minute onboarding chat and we select 24 homes matched to your life — each with a 2-sentence explanation of why it fits, an honest Nestimate valuation with a price range and confidence level, and real walk, climate, and school signals.

    For any US residential address, we also run a deep analysis using 6 parallel AI domain experts in under 2 minutes. The experts cover financial, market, physical, environmental, neighborhood, and legal dimensions. A synthesizer then produces a composite score and an honest summary.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is Nestlyze different from Zillow, Redfin, or my agent?">
    Zillow and Redfin are listing databases with black-box valuation models. Your local agent is typically commission-paid by the seller and knows one sub-region well.

    Nestlyze is built for the buyer:

    - **No commission** — Nestlyze never takes money from a seller or seller's agent. Revenue comes entirely from buyer-paid credits and subscriptions.
    - **Transparent reasoning** — every Nestimate comes with 5–8 reasoning bullets explaining what drove the number. You see the comps, condition signals, and photo analysis.
    - **Cross-region recommendations** — the AI recommends homes across regions a local agent might never show you, because it has no territory bias.
    - **Buyer-side analysis** — the 6 AI agents are explicitly designed to find problems, not to sell you on the property.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Nestlyze free?">
    Your first deep property analysis is free — no credit card required. After that, analyses cost credits.

    The following features are always free, with no credits needed:

    - AI recommendations (your 24 matched homes)
    - Ask-AI follow-up questions
    - Nestimate valuation on listing cards
    - Browsing listings and the map
    - Shortlist and compare

    When you need more analyses, credit packs start at $9.99 for 5 analyses. See the [credits and pricing page](/account/credits-pricing) for current pack details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Nestlyze represent the seller?">
    No. Nestlyze never takes a commission from a seller or a seller's agent. Our entire revenue comes from buyers — through credits and subscriptions.

    This matters because most real estate tools you use today are ultimately paid by the people selling you the house. Nestlyze has no financial incentive to make any particular property look more appealing than it is.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Nestlyze without creating an account?">
    You can run one free analysis without creating an account. The free trial is limited to one analysis per IP address.

    Creating a free account unlocks your reports history, saved searches, shortlist, AI recommendations, and the referral program. Sign-up requires only an email and password, or you can use Google OAuth.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get my credits refunded if the address isn't residential?">
    Nestlyze automatically detects commercial properties before running an analysis. If the address you enter is not residential — an office building, retail space, or industrial property — the analysis stops before using your credit and the credit is returned to your balance immediately.

    You don't need to contact support. The refund is automatic and instant.

    If a full analysis fails for a different reason (for example, a data source is unavailable and the report cannot be generated), your credit is also automatically refunded.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?">
    Nestlyze currently runs as a web app at nestlyze.com, which is optimized for mobile browsers. There is no native iOS or Android app at this time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
