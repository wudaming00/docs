---
title: "Nestlyze Coverage: Cities, States, and Availability"
description: "Nestlyze's personalized recommendation pool covers 3,100+ Bay Area listings. Single-address deep analysis works for any US residential address nationwide."
---

Nestlyze has two distinct coverage areas: the recommendation pool (a curated set of active listings used for personalized picks) and single-address deep analysis (available for any US residential address). The two work independently — you can analyze any home nationwide even if it's outside the recommendation pool cities.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which cities does Nestlyze cover for recommendations?">
    The AI recommendation pool currently covers the San Francisco Bay Area, with 3,100+ active and recently sold listings. Covered cities include:

    - San Jose
    - Oakland
    - Fremont
    - Sunnyvale
    - Palo Alto
    - Berkeley
    - And more across the broader Bay Area

    When you complete the onboarding chat, your 24 personalized recommendations are drawn from this pool and ranked against your buyer profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does deep analysis work outside the Bay Area?">
    Yes. Deep property analysis works for **any US residential address**, regardless of whether it's in the recommendation pool. Enter any address in the search bar and Nestlyze will run the full 6-agent analysis — financial, market, physical, environmental, neighborhood, and legal.

    Data availability for some signals (such as school attendance boundaries and certain contamination databases) is stronger in California, but the core analysis pipeline works nationwide.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When will Nestlyze expand to new cities?">
    The recommendation pool expansion roadmap includes:

    - Austin, TX
    - Seattle, WA
    - New York metro area
    - Miami, FL

    No specific launch dates are confirmed for these markets. Deep analysis is already available for properties in all of these cities today — expansion means adding them to the curated recommendation pool so you'll receive personalized picks from those markets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are school attendance boundaries available everywhere?">
    School attendance boundary data from the federal School Attendance Boundary Survey is most complete for California. Nestlyze performs precise geographic lookups against these boundaries to identify the exact school zone for a given address.

    Outside California, school zone assignment relies on GreatSchools data, which lists nearby schools but may be less precise about exact attendance boundaries. The report clearly indicates which method was used for a given address.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I analyze a commercial property?">
    No. Nestlyze is designed exclusively for US residential properties. When you submit an address, the system checks whether it is residential before running the analysis.

    If the address is detected as commercial — an office building, retail space, industrial property, or other non-residential use — the analysis stops immediately and your credit is automatically refunded. You are not charged for a report that cannot be completed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Nestlyze work for condos and townhomes?">
    Yes. Deep analysis works for any US residential property type, including:

    - Single-family homes
    - Condos
    - Townhomes
    - Multi-family residences

    For condos and townhomes, the Legal agent includes HOA document review signals and flags any indicators of underfunded reserves or pending special assessments when that information is available in public records or listing disclosures.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
