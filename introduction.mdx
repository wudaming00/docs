Nestlyze is an AI buyer's agent built for US homebuyers. Most mainstream real estate tools are listing databases first and advisors second — they're optimized for browsing inventory, not for helping you decide. Nestlyze is positioned differently: it represents the buyer, surfaces transparent reasoning behind every number, and is willing to look outside the geographic boundaries a local agent typically covers.

## How Nestlyze is different

### Aligned with the buyer

Nestlyze is funded by buyers through credits and subscriptions, not by seller-side commissions. That alignment shapes the product: recommendations and valuations are tuned to help you decide, not to close a transaction.

### Glass-box valuations (Nestimate)

Every Nestimate is a price range with a confidence read — not a single number. Each valuation includes 5–8 reasoning bullets that reference listing photos, comparable sales, school quality, and physical condition signals, so you can see what the model is weighing.

### Cross-region search

Recommendations aren't anchored to a single neighborhood or ZIP. If a home two cities over fits your commute, budget, and school requirements better than what's nearby, Nestlyze will surface it.

### Fair Housing guardrails by design

Nestlyze's models are explicitly forbidden from reasoning about protected classes (race, religion, national origin, family status, disability, sex) or demographic proxies such as "family-oriented neighborhood." Every explanation cites only housing factors: price, schools (by rating), commute time, and physical condition.
