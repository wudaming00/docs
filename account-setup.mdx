Creating a Nestlyze account takes under 5 minutes and gives you immediate access to your first free property deep analysis — no credit card required. You can sign up with a Google account for one-click access, or register with any email address and a password of your choosing. Once your account is active, your free credit is waiting and you can run your first report straight away.

<Note>
  Every account receives 1 free property analysis. This credit is applied automatically — you will not be asked for payment until you purchase an additional credit pack.
</Note>

## Sign up and verify your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to nestlyze.com and open the sign-in page">
    Navigate to [nestlyze.com](https://nestlyze.com) and click **Sign in** in the top navigation, or click **Try Nestlyze** from any page in the docs. The sign-in page shows two options: **Continue with Google** and the email/password form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your sign-up method">
    **Option A — Google OAuth (recommended for speed)**

    Click **Continue with Google**. You will be redirected to Google's OAuth consent screen. Select the Google account you want to use, grant Nestlyze access to your name and email address, and you will be returned to Nestlyze and logged in immediately. No email verification step is required.

    **Option B — Email and password**

    Click **Sign up** below the sign-in form to switch to registration mode. Fill in three fields:

    - **Name** — your display name inside the app
    - **Email** — the address where Nestlyze will send your verification email and any report notifications
    - **Password** — minimum 6 characters; use something you do not reuse elsewhere

    Then click **Create account**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email address (email sign-up only)">
    If you registered with email and password, Nestlyze sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open the email and click the confirmation link. The link is valid for 24 hours. Once verified, your account is fully active and your free analysis credit is applied.

    <Tip>
      Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within a few minutes. The sender address is **hello@nestlyze.com**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the onboarding chat (optional but recommended)">
    After logging in you will see an option to complete a short onboarding chat — around 2 minutes. The chat collects your commute location, budget range, must-have features, school requirements, and lifestyle priorities. Nestlyze uses this profile to generate your 24 personalized home recommendations.

    You can skip onboarding and go straight to a property analysis, but completing it unlocks the AI recommendations feature.

    <Info>
      Your onboarding profile is encrypted at rest and read only by the AI models ranking homes for you. No Nestlyze employee reads individual profiles, and your profile is never shared with sellers, listing agents, or any third party.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run your first free property analysis">
    From the home screen, enter any US residential address in the search bar and click **Analyze**. Nestlyze runs 6 AI domain agents in parallel — Financial, Market, Physical, Environmental, Neighborhood, and Legal — and returns a synthesized report with a composite score in under 2 minutes. Your free credit is consumed on your first analysis.

    <Tip>
      Use your free analysis on a home you are seriously considering. The report includes total cost of ownership projections, hazard layers (flood, fire, earthquake), school boundary lookups with accurate polygon matching, comparable sales, and permit signals.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the free trial includes

Your free credit covers one full deep analysis report. The following features are free regardless of credit balance:

- Browsing listings on the map and in list view
- Running the onboarding chat and viewing your AI recommendations
- Viewing Nestimate valuation ranges on recommendation cards
- Adding homes to your shortlist
- Asking the AI follow-up questions about recommended properties

Deep analyses — where Nestlyze pulls live data and runs 6 parallel AI agents against a specific address — each cost one credit. Your first is free.

## Find your credit balance

After logging in, click your avatar or name in the top-right corner and select **Settings**. Your current credit balance is shown at the top of the **Credits & billing** section. From the same page you can purchase additional credit packs (10 analyses for $9.99) or view your analysis history.

<Info>
  Each Nestlyze account also gets a unique referral code in **Settings**. Every friend who signs up using your referral link earns you +1 additional credit, with no cap on how many you can earn.
</Info>

## Reset your password

If you signed up with email and password and need to reset it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [nestlyze.com](https://nestlyze.com) and click **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Forgot password'">
    Below the password field, click the **Forgot password?** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address associated with your account and submit the form. Nestlyze sends a password-reset link to that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Open the email from **hello@nestlyze.com** and click the reset link. Enter and confirm your new password. Your session will be active immediately after.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you signed up with **Continue with Google**, there is no Nestlyze password to reset. Your authentication is managed by Google — update your Google account password through Google's account settings if needed.
</Warning>
